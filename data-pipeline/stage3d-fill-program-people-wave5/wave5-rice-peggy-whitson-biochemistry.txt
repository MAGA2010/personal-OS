Rice Biosciences’ alumna Peggy Whitson ’86, America’s most experienced astronaut, will deliver Rice’s undergraduate commencement address at 7 p.m. May 4 at Rice Stadium.
Her storied career in Space City Houston began at Rice, where she studied as a Robert A. Welch Predoctoral Fellow and earned a Ph.D. in biochemistry.
